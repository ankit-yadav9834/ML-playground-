India is a sovereign, democratic republic in South Asia and the world’s most populous country. It became independent from British rule in 1947 and adopted its Constitution in 1950.

Capital: New Delhi

Government: Parliamentary democracy

Languages: Hindi & English (official), plus 22 scheduled languages

Currency: Indian Rupee (₹)

India has one of the oldest continuous civilizations, with major contributions in mathematics (zero), science, philosophy, medicine (Ayurveda), and astronomy.

Its economy is a mixed economy and among the top 5 globally by GDP (PPP), with strengths in IT, agriculture, manufacturing, space technology, and services.

India is known for its cultural diversity, religious plurality, and geographic range — from the Himalayas to coastal plains and deserts.

Strategically, India is a key global power, a member of G20, BRICS, and an emerging leader in technology, defense, and space (ISRO).
